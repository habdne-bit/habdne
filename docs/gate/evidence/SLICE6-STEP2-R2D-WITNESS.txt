4. THE DEFECT, AND THE FIX
--------------------------
  The witness claimed "C waits on U" but allowed C a FIXED 8 s to reach the
  lock. A stall unrelated to the lock can use the window up while the
  behaviour is right. The fix gives the witness the semantics it states:
  until a backend waits on the holder, or the waiting thread ENDS, with a
  60 s safety cap. It still discriminates: without the lock (mutations L1,
  L2, L3) the thread ends at once and the witness is False at once.
  The Slice 5 tests that share this 8 s pattern are not changed by this
  step; the same fragility is stated for them in the delivery note.
