What each identifier's length costs (measured on a text message, bytes per

  wamid            2
  from             1

Reading:
- The room is the limit less the envelope with one byte of content, plus that byte.
- A content's bytes are its serialized bytes: a 2-, 3- or 4-byte UTF-8 character costs
  2, 3 or 4; " and \ cost 2; a control character costs 2 (\n, \t...) or 6 (\u00XX).
- For image, video and document, the caption shares the room with nothing else that
  varies here; the media id is fixed at the example's length.
- For audio and sticker the only content is the media id, Meta's identifier.
- R1-T7.7: real envelopes are not measured. No real traffic exists (R1-D13 (a)).
