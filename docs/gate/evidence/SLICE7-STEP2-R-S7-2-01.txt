    with FOR KEY SHARE on the thread row; the message takes FOR UPDATE on it.
    The two conflict (PostgreSQL 16, §13.3.2, Table 13.3), in both orders.
  - Order B: the message's consent check is a later statement than its
    lock; under READ COMMITTED it reads the binding as committed when the
    wait ended (PostgreSQL 16, §13.2.1). A committed binding is therefore
    SEEN, and the content archived.
  - So the delivery of f7e5297 (§3, reading 6) and the docstring of
    services/communication.py were WRONG: they said the binding table had no
    foreign key to the thread and that a binding in flight is not waited
    for. Both are corrected after this record. The review of f7e5297
    inferred this from the schema, the code and the lock table, unmeasured;
    this measures it. These are our runs.
