Result: in each of the three runs, the two FINAL cases fail, and in the same
way:
- final-same-body: B is 409 MATCH_REVIEW_DECIDED, not A's response;
- final-other-body: B is 409 MATCH_REVIEW_DECIDED, not IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_CONFLICT.
The witness held in every case: the test asserts it before releasing A, so a
case that reached its answer had it.
The three controls pass:
- when A's decision is NMI, B passes `prepare`, and its claim meets A's
  committed key (23505), which already replays or conflicts;
- with another key, B is refused as decided, and its key is not consumed.

Cause (stated by the review by reading; confirmed by the above):
`CommandService.run` looks the key up BEFORE `prepare`. A refusal raised by
`prepare` after the lock wait leaves `run` before the key is looked at again.
