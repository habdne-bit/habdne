  M8  CONFIRMS the conflict the review of dcebe46 suspected. A STRICT guard
      freezing a thread's links also refuses the FK's ON DELETE SET NULL:
      deleting the thread's party is refused (parties have no delete guard,

      lets the FK act, but also accepts a DIRECT UPDATE of a link to NULL.
      The guard sees pg_trigger_depth() = 1 for a direct UPDATE and 2 under
      the FK's referential action; an FK-ONLY guard (NULL allowed only at
      depth > 1) refuses both direct changes and lets the FK act. Its stated
      limit: depth > 1 is also true for an UPDATE issued from inside another
      trigger; the threads table has none today (M3).
